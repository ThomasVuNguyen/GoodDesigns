# Motion notes: 23-Year-Old Rejected $250K To Build a Fund for South Asia

Lovable · 02:23.96 · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZ-9G07Wjws

The film runs on two kinds of movement. In the studio the camera is locked off, and everything that moves is laid over the live action: captions that build a word or two at a time in the empty wall around Zain, and a few small props that appear on cue (a cheque, two flags, a handful of color swatches). In the narrator's world nothing is cut in cleanly. Photographs bloom out of creased paper like ink spreading in water, gold capitals type on word by word, and the app arrives as a phone, a wordmark that changes script and a card dropped onto the page. The sequences below sample each kind.

## How to read this

Each sequence is a run of frames sampled at a fixed rate, listed in order with its timestamp, so stepping through the files is a flip book of the move. The bar after each frame is the motion energy from it to the next frame: the mean change in RGB between consecutive source frames (24 fps, downscaled to 96x54), on a log scale from the film's noise floor to its 99th percentile. ▁ is a hold, █ is a full-frame wipe or the fastest movement in the film, and a small shape gliding across a flat field sits around ▃–▅. A run like ▁▂▅█▅▂▁ is an ease-in-out; █▅▃▂▁ is an ease-out that lands and settles. `trail.webp`, where present, is an onion skin of the sequence: moving shapes from every frame over the static background, earlier positions fainter.
