# Motion notes: History of Search

Notion · 00:30.16 · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=siOeaIJ-Ldg

Almost all the movement is cutting. The first fifteen seconds are still images held for under a second each, and the motion that matters sits in three places: a whip pan across a tapestry that hands the montage to the Round Table, an unbroken product take that pushes into the search box and types the query at headline size, and a closing move that shrinks the interface onto a yellow card before the wordmark.

## How to read this

Each sequence is a run of frames sampled at a fixed rate, listed in order with its timestamp, so stepping through the files is a flip book of the move. The bar after each frame is the motion energy from it to the next frame: the mean change in RGB between consecutive source frames (24 fps, downscaled to 96x54), on a log scale from the film's noise floor to its 99th percentile. ▁ is a hold, █ is a full-frame wipe or the fastest movement in the film, and a small shape gliding across a flat field sits around ▃–▅. A run like ▁▂▅█▅▂▁ is an ease-in-out; █▅▃▂▁ is an ease-out that lands and settles. `trail.webp`, where present, is an onion skin of the sequence: moving shapes from every frame over the static background, earlier positions fainter.

00:17.20–00:21.20 (4.00 s) · 25 frames at 6 fps, 0.167 s apart · push in, type-on, follow the cursor, loading state

'Project Camelot' is then typed one or two letters a frame at display size, and the text slides left to keep the cursor in frame.
