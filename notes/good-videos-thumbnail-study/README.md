# Good Videos thumbnail study

This folder separates the 22 saved GoodDesigns video thumbnails into three things:

- `original/`: copies of the source thumbnails in `gallery/thumbs/videos/`.
- `text-only/`: transcriptions of the prominent display copy, plus source and scene notes.
- `image-only/`: edited clean plates with the prominent overlaid lettering removed. The three originals with no added display copy are copied unchanged.
- `overviews/`: three numbered contact sheets each for the originals and clean plates.

Open [index.html](index.html) for the side-by-side review. [inventory.csv](inventory.csv) is the sortable list of on-image words, subjects, and composition.

## What “image-only” means here

The clean plates were made with the built-in image editing tool. Covered areas are **AI reconstructions**; they are not hidden original pixels recovered from a layered source. The original JPEGs are flat images. Small text that belongs to a photographed app interface or a physical product label remains part of the scene. These are study assets; the originals remain available beside every reconstruction.

## First distinctions to discuss

The set includes human stories, product demonstrations, cinematic reveals, and research or process footage. Several use a short outcome phrase over a person in a real setting. Others rely on a singular object, a before/after comparison, or a character without an explanatory headline. We should decide which examples match the desired audience and video format before turning these observations into a formula.
